Privacy Policy — Roots

Last updated: May 2026

Roots is a private family history app. Your data is only shared with family members you invite — never sold or shared with third parties.

What we collect

  • Your email address (for sign-in)
  • Your name and profile photo
  • Family information you enter: names, dates, places, stories, and photos of family members
  • Device platform and app version (for bug reports you submit)

How it's stored

All data is stored securely using Supabase, hosted on AWS. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Who can see your data

Only family members you invite to Roots can see the information you add. We do not share your data with advertisers or any third parties.
