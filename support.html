Support — Roots

Roots is a private, invite-only app for collecting and sharing your family's history — stories, photos, a family tree, life events, and recipes, visible only to the family members you invite.

Need help?

Email matt.j.clary@gmail.com with any questions, bug reports, or feedback, and we'll get back to you. You can also use the in-app feedback form from Settings → Send Feedback.

Common questions

  • How do I join my family's Roots? You'll need an invite from a family member who's already using the app. If you were sent a claim link, open it on your phone to get started.
  • How do I sign in? Roots uses email sign-in only — enter your email and we'll send you a one-time code, no password needed.
  • Who can see what I post? Only the family members you invite to Roots. You can also control visibility per story or entry.
  • How do I delete my account or export my data? Go to Settings → Privacy & Data in the app, or see our account deletion page.
